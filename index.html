A first visit here isn't a quick prescription hand-off — your history, your habits, and how your body responds to things all matter to the remedy we choose.

With over 20 years of experience, Dr. Ramya has treated a wide range of conditions including orthopedic complaints, allergies and asthma, rheumatology, and viral illnesses. Her practice today is focused mainly on orthopedic cases — joint pain, back pain, arthritis, and related mobility issues — alongside continued care for allergy, asthma, and rheumatic conditions.

Medicines are dispensed on-site in small glass vials, with clear instructions on dosage and diet to follow alongside treatment.
